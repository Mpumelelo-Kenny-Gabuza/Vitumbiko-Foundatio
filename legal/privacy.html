1. Who we are

Vitumbiko Foundation Limited is a company limited by guarantee registered in Malawi (Reg. No. COYG-Q4AFV5L), with its registered office at Mzuzu Town at Chenda Holdings, Chitheka Village, T/A Kabunduli, Mzimba District.

2. What we collect

  • Information you submit through our contact, volunteer, partner, and donation forms (name, email, phone, message).
  • Basic, anonymised analytics about how the site is used.
  • Donation transaction details processed by our payment providers (PayChangu, Stripe). We do not store card numbers.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries.
  • To process donations and issue receipts.
  • To improve our programs and website.
  • To meet legal and reporting obligations.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers necessary to run the organisation (payment processors, form handlers, hosting), and where required by law.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing info@vitumbikofoundation.org.

6. Cookies

This site uses no tracking cookies by default. If we later add analytics, this section will be updated.

Questions about this policy: info@vitumbikofoundation.org.
