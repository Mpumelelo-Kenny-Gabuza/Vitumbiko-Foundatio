Safeguarding Policy

Our commitment to protecting the people we serve.

Placeholder — Vitumbiko Foundation works with women, young people, and communities. A safeguarding policy is essential. Have this reviewed by a qualified practitioner and adapted to your operating context before launch.

1. Purpose

This policy sets out our commitment to protecting all beneficiaries, staff, volunteers, and partners from harm, abuse, and exploitation.

2. Scope

Applies to all directors, staff, volunteers, contractors, and partners acting on behalf of Vitumbiko Foundation.

3. Principles

  • The welfare of the person is paramount.
  • All people, regardless of age, gender, or background, have the right to protection from harm.
  • Confidentiality is respected, within the limits of safety and the law.
  • Concerns are taken seriously and acted upon promptly.

4. Code of conduct

  • No inappropriate physical, sexual, or emotional contact with beneficiaries.
  • No exploitation of any kind, including financial or material.
  • No discrimination on any grounds.
  • No use of alcohol or illegal substances while on duty.
  • Compliance with all applicable Malawian law.

5. Reporting a concern

Anyone may report a safeguarding concern by contacting the designated safeguarding lead at info@vitumbikofoundation.org or by phone on +265 999 635 556. Concerns may be raised anonymously.

6. Response

All concerns are recorded, reviewed, and — where appropriate — referred to the relevant authorities. We cooperate fully with any investigation.

7. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually by the directors.
