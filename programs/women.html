Empowering Women

Training local women in vocational and financial literacy skills, enabling them to start their own businesses.

The challenge

Many women in rural Mzimba have the drive and the ideas to start businesses but lack access to practical training, financial literacy, and start-up support. Placeholder paragraph — expand with real context from the field.

What we do

  • Vocational training — practical, market-relevant skills taught in small groups.
  • Financial literacy — budgeting, saving, pricing, record-keeping, and accessing credit.
  • Business mentorship — ongoing support as participants launch and grow.
