Youth Development

A youth digital innovation hub training young Malawians in digital marketing and tech skills.

The challenge

Young people in Mzimba are entering a job market that increasingly demands digital skills, yet access to training and technology is limited. Placeholder — expand.

What we do

  • Digital marketing training — social media, content, and online business basics.
  • Tech skills — computer literacy, productivity tools, and introductory coding.
  • Innovation hub — a physical space with devices and internet for learning and collaboration.

How it works

Placeholder — describe cohort structure, duration, equipment available, and mentorship.

Who it's for

Young people in Mzimba District. Placeholder — refine age range and eligibility.

Outcomes

  • Placeholder — partner placements

How you can support
