Critical Skills Training

Practical, life-ready skills that help participants navigate work, money, and daily life with confidence.

The challenge

Many young people and adults in Mzimba leave school without the practical skills needed for work, entrepreneurship, or managing daily life. Critical Skills Training fills that gap with hands-on, immediately useful training.

What we do

  • Life skills — communication, decision-making, problem-solving, and self-awareness.
  • Employability skills — CV writing, interview preparation, and workplace conduct.
  • Financial literacy — budgeting, saving, avoiding debt traps, and basic bookkeeping.
  • Digital literacy — basic computer use, internet safety, and using a smartphone for work.
  • Health and safety — first aid basics, hygiene, and personal safety.

How you can support

Your donation funds training materials, facilitator stipends, and participant kits.
