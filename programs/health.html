Health & Wellness

Monthly mental health workshops and confidential counseling sessions, delivered with dignity.

The challenge

Mental health services are scarce in rural Malawi, and stigma often prevents people from seeking help. Placeholder — expand with real context.

What we do

  • Monthly mental health workshops — community sessions on stress, grief, trauma, and coping.
  • Confidential counseling — one-on-one sessions with trained counselors, in private.
  • Referrals — connection to clinical services where needed.

How it works

Who it's for

Anyone in the communities we serve. Priority to women and youth. Placeholder — refine.
