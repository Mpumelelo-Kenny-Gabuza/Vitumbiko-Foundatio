About Us

A foundation built in Mzimba, for Malawi

Vitumbiko Foundation Limited is a non-profit company limited by guarantee, incorporated on 28 August 2026 under the Companies Act, 2013.

Our story

Vitumbiko Foundation was founded by a small group of Malawians who saw first-hand how women, youth, mental health and critical skills were being underserved in rural Mzimba. Rather than wait for outside solutions, they registered a company limited by guarantee — a legal form that locks the organisation's income to its charitable objects and forbids distribution to members.

From our base at Chenda Holdings in Mzuzu Town, we run programs across Chitheka Village and the surrounding areas of T/A Kabunduli.

Mission

To empower women, nurture health and wellness, and equip young people with the skills they need to build dignified, self-determined lives.

Vision

A Malawi where every woman, young person, and family has access to the skills, care, and opportunity they need to thrive.

Values

  • Community first. Programs are designed with, not for, the people we serve.
  • Dignity. Every interaction respects the worth of the individual.
  • Confidentiality. Especially in counseling and safeguarding matters.
  • Accountability. Every kwacha is applied to our stated objects.
  • Sustainability. We build skills that outlast our programs.
